## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Transforms the sector universe into a complete institutional rotation radar covering **62 specialized industry clusters** across the NSE 500, BSE 500, and marquee 2024–2026 IPOs, with real 63-day NSE/BSE closing price trajectories pre-verified and cached for instant 0ms rendering.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Active institutional and positional equity traders tracking sector rotation, relative strength vs. NIFTY 50 / Midcap 100 / Smallcap 100, and intra-sector breadth tightening.
- **Key Value**: Standard exchange indices (like Nifty FMCG or Nifty IT) lump slow legacy giants together with high-growth challengers, hiding real rotation. Splitting `Dairy & Value-Added Food` from `Packaged FMCG`, or `Quick Commerce & Consumer Tech` from `IT Services`, immediately surfaces where institutional capital is flowing.

## 3. User Experience & Visual Design

- **Key User Flows**:
  1. **Macro Cluster Discovery (Left Sidebar)**: Filter all 62 sectors by thematic category pill (`All`, `New-Age & Tech`, `Consumer & Retail`, `Energy & Infra`, `Financials`, `Manufacturing & Healthcare`) or search by `@ticker` / company name (e.g., typing `@swiggy`, `@eternal`, or `@milkymist` immediately highlights its parent sector).
  2. **Equal-Weight vs. Market-Cap Weight Toggle**: In the top chart proof bar, switch between `Equal-Weighted` (default, where each constituent contributes equally to cluster momentum) and `Market-Cap Weighted` (highlighting whether large-cap anchors or mid/small-cap challengers are leading).
  3. **Multi-Stock Co-Movement & Dispersion Proof**: View all 6–10 constituent trajectories normalized to Base 100 against NIFTY 50, Midcap 100, Smallcap 100, and NIFTY 500.
- **Visual Identity & Theme**:
  - Preserves the existing high-contrast institutional palette (`#f4f3ee` paper canvas, `#08090a` charcoal ink, `#2563eb` active cluster focus, `#10b981` advancing emerald, `#f43f5e` declining rose) and `Outfit` + `Plus Jakarta Sans` + `JetBrains Mono` (`tabular-nums`) typography.

## 4. Technical Architecture & Data Strategy

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 62-Cluster Institutional Taxonomy                        │
│   (NSE 500 + BSE 500 + 2024–2026 New-Age & Thematic IPO Constituents)    │
└────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┘
                                     │
                                     ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│         Yahoo Finance v8 / NSE-BSE Historical Sync Pipeline              │
│  • Resolves NSE (.NS) & BSE (.BO) symbols + corporate rebrand aliases    │
│    (e.g., ETERNAL -> ZOMATO.NS/ETERNAL.NS, MILKYMIST, SWIGGY.NS)         │
│  • Pre-seeds 63-day daily closes into verifiedLiveMarketData.json        │
└────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┘
                                     │
                                     ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 IndexTrackerView & Rotation Engine                       │
│  • Left Sidebar: Category Quick-Filter + 62 Clusters + @Company Search   │
│  • Right Canvas: Equal-Wt / MCap-Wt Toggle + Multi-Stock Ribbon Overlay  │
│  • Breadth Spectrum: 1W Return Dispersion + Synchrony Verdict            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Symbol Resolution & Rebrand Handling**:
  - Automatically maps rebranded or newly listed tickers (such as `ETERNAL` ↔ `ETERNAL.NS` / `ZOMATO.NS`, `SWIGGY.NS`, `FIRSTCRY.NS`, `OLAELEC.NS`, `WAAREEENER.NS`, `PREMIERENE.NS`, `MILKYMIST.NS` / `.BO`) in `STOCK_SYMBOL_MAP` so 100% of constituents have verified 63-day historical price series.
- **Zero-Latency Pre-Seeding**:
  - Batch-fetches and validates the entire expanded constituent universe into `verifiedLiveMarketData.json` at build time so switching across any of the 62 sectors renders all constituent lines instantaneously.
